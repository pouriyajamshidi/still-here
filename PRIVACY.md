# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: September 24, 2026_

This policy covers the **YouTube Still Here** browser extension.

## What data is collected

None. The extension does not collect, store, or share any personal or usage data.

## What the extension does

It runs only on `youtube.com` pages. There, it clicks the "Video paused. Continue watching?" button when that dialog appears and keeps the page from looking idle. All of this happens locally in your browser.

The extension:

- does not send anything over the network
- does not use cookies, storage, or analytics
- does not read your browsing history, account, or the videos you watch
- asks for no extra browser permissions
